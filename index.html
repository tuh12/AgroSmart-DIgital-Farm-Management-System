Project overview

Built for modern agriculture and future farming

This project is based on smart farming principles. It combines crop recommendation, field health monitoring, daily task tracking, complaint management, and leaf disease analysis to help farmers work more efficiently and make better decisions.

What this system is based on

  • Crop knowledge and vegetable growth patterns
  • Soil, weather, and field condition analysis
  • Disease and pest identification from leaf images
  • Daily farm planning, action tracking, and support
  • AI assistance for future smart agriculture innovation
